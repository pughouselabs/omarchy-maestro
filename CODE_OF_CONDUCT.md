# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming and respectful community. Participants should
be considerate, use inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, and unwelcome sexual
attention are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation in response to behavior they consider inappropriate.

Report problems privately to the project maintainer. The maintainer will review
reports and decide on a response appropriate to the circumstances.
